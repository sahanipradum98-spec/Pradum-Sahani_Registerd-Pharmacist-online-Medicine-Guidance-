🍎 Diet & Precaution Guide

Diabetes

Eat: Apple, Guava, Papaya, Orange, Pear
Avoid: Mango, Grapes, Sugarcane juice
Precaution: Avoid sweets & walk daily.

Blood Pressure

Eat: Pomegranate, Watermelon, Kiwi
Avoid: Too much salt, oily food
Precaution: Stay hydrated, control sodium.

Thyroid

Eat: Berries, Apples, Kiwi
Avoid: Raw cabbage, cauliflower
Precaution: Take thyroid medicine empty stomach.

Loose Motion / Diarrhea

Eat: Banana, Rice, Toast, ORS, Coconut water
Avoid: Milk, spicy & oily food
Precaution: Stay hydrated; consult pharmacist if severe.

Vomiting

Eat: Small frequent sips of water, Ginger tea
Avoid: Junk food, alcohol
Precaution: Rest well; hydrate slowly.

Kidney Patient

Eat: Apple, Pineapple, Berries
Avoid: Banana, Tomato, Salt-rich food
Precaution: Limit protein and potassium-rich diet.
